3. SMS / Text Messaging

Consent. By providing your mobile phone number and checking the applicable opt-in box, you agree to receive text messages from Paid Coaching. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase or of applying to any program. You may provide your phone number without opting in to SMS.

Separate consent categories. We request consent separately for each of the following:

  • Transactional / conversational messages: appointment confirmations, reminders, follow-ups, and replies related to your application or enrollment.
  • Promotional / marketing messages: offers, program announcements, and other marketing content.

Opting in to one category does not opt you in to the other.

Message frequency. Message frequency varies. You can expect to receive up to approximately 4–8 messages per month, depending on your activity and the programs you have enrolled in.

Cost. Message and data rates may apply. These charges come from your mobile carrier, not from us.

Opting out. You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will send a confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. To rejoin, sign up again as you did originally or reply START.

Help. For assistance, reply HELP to any message, or contact us at Tom@educatr.co.

Carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Related terms. Our SMS program is also governed by our Terms of Service, available at paidcoaching.com.

With trusted vendors who perform services on our behalf, such as our CRM, scheduling tools (e.g., Calendly), payment processors, email platforms, and SMS messaging providers, strictly so they can operate and deliver our services to you. These providers are contractually restricted from using your information for any other purpose, and mobile phone numbers and SMS consent data are shared only with the messaging provider needed to deliver your messages.

We do not share your information for any purpose outside of the categories above.
