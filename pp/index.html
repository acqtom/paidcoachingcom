This Privacy Policy explains how Helmbury International FZ-LLC ("Helmbury", "we", "us" or "our"), which operates Paidcoaching.com, collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit our website, book a call with us, or use our services.

By using our website or services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you fill in a form, book a strategy call, apply to work with us or become a client, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your business name, website, social media profiles and details about your coaching offer
  • Business information such as revenue, ad spend, sales figures and marketing performance that you choose to share

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we and our partners may automatically collect your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, time spent on pages, the page that referred you, and similar usage data. We collect this using cookies, pixels and similar technologies.

Payment information. Payments are handled by third-party payment processors. We do not store your full card details on our systems.

  • To schedule, confirm and hold strategy calls, including contacting you by phone, SMS and email
  • To provide our services, including building funnels, writing ads, VSL scripts and email sequences, and managing campaigns
  • To send you information about our services, results and offers
  • To measure and improve our website, advertising and marketing
  • To process payments and manage our client relationships

Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP, and unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email. Consent to receive marketing messages is not a condition of purchase.

We use cookies and tracking technologies, including advertising pixels from platforms such as Meta and Google, to understand how visitors use our website, measure the performance of our ads, and show relevant ads to people who have visited our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings and opt out of interest-based advertising through the settings of the relevant ad platforms.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:
